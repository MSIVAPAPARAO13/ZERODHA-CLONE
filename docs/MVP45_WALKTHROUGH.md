# MVP-45 Walkthrough — Market Narrative Engine

## Overview
MVP-45 introduces the **Market Narrative Engine** into TradeFlow, synthesizing discrete market events, observable regimes, sector breadth, and portfolio context into a structured, evidence-grounded market narrative.

## Mandatory 5-Section Narrative Structure
1. **What Changed?**: Observable market regime transitions, advance/decline breadth ratios, and universe volatility shifts backed by explicit citations.
2. **What is Unusual?**: Critical and significant event spikes, volume surges, and volatility outliers.
3. **Which Sectors Changed?**: Sector-level directional momentum and advancing breadths across verified taxonomy.
4. **Which Monitored Assets Were Affected?**: Explicit intersection between active market events and user portfolio holdings.
5. **What Remains Unknown?**: Honest accounting of data limitations (e.g. block deals, corporate action registries, earnings calendars).

## Guardrails Verified
- **Zero Speculative / Predictive Language**: Guaranteed absence of promises, price targets, or financial advice.
- **Evidence-Based Observation**: Disclaimed explicitly as `"EVIDENCE_BASED_OBSERVATION — NOT A PREDICTION OR RECOMMENDATION"`.
- **Multi-Tenant User Isolation**: No leakage between user portfolios or event feeds.
- **Zero Financial Ledger Mutation**: Completely non-mutating intelligence layer.
