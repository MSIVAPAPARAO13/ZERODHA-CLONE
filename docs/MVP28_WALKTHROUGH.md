# MVP-28 Walkthrough: Adaptive Strategy Lab

## 1. Strategy Health
Welcome to the Adaptive Strategy Lab. You can now select any of your Playbooks and instantly see its complete evidence profile. It compares your initial Validation metrics directly against your live Forward execution. If your average outcome is dropping, the Lab flags an "Evidence Deviation."

## 2. Walk-Forward & Sensitivities
Never curve-fit again. You can run a Walk-Forward test that chronologically steps through history (Train -> Validate -> Forward) to ensure your strategy didn't just get lucky in one specific month. Additionally, you can run Parameter Sensitivity experiments to see if a volume requirement of 1.5x is a stable edge or if 1.6x completely destroys the strategy (indicating severe overfitting).

## 3. Regime Matrix
Does your strategy only work when volatility is high? The Lab maps your trades into historical Regimes, proving mathematically where your strategy excels and where it historically bleeds capital.

## 4. Controlled Experiments
Run Strategy A vs Strategy B using identical historical periods and execution assumptions. TradeFlow lays out the empirical differences so you can iteratively update your Playbooks based on facts, not guesswork.
