# MVP-27 Walkthrough: Market Intelligence & Discovery

## 1. The Market Radar
TradeFlow no longer just waits for you to type in a symbol. The new **Market Radar** actively scans your Watchlist. It detects unusual volume deviations and flags significant price context changes using historical baselines pulled cleanly from the Market Data Provider.

## 2. Playbook Match
The most powerful feature of the Radar is **Playbook Matching**. TradeFlow automatically checks the current market against the rules of your active, validated Playbooks. If RELIANCE meets 4 out of 5 entry conditions for your "Breakout v3" playbook, it surfaces immediately on your dashboard as a Playbook Match.

## 3. Research Opportunities
Instead of generic "BUY" signals, the Radar produces **Research Opportunities**. You can instantly click "Investigate" on an unusual activity alert, linking you directly into the Strategy Validation lab to test if that exact setup historically generates a reliable edge.

## 4. Natural Language Scans
You can type "Show me stocks moving up on high volume" into the scanner. The TradeFlow AI translates that plain English into strict, deterministic rules for the Condition Engine. The scan is then run deterministically against real market data—guaranteeing no AI hallucinations in the final evidence.
