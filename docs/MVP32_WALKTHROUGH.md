# MVP-32 Walkthrough: Market Impact Intelligence

## 1. Context Over Noise
You are flooded with market news every day. TradeFlow's Market Impact Intelligence cuts out the noise by only showing you the events that mathematically intersect with your financial state, strategies, and research.

## 2. The Impact Graph
When a market event drops (e.g., "TCS Earnings Released"), click on it. TradeFlow instantly calculates that TCS makes up 22% of your portfolio, affects 3 active Playbooks, overlaps with 1 price alert, and ties back to a Research Hypothesis you wrote 3 weeks ago.

## 3. "Why Does This Matter?"
By clicking the "Why Does This Matter?" button, the AI Explainer reads the deterministic impact graph. It will never tell you to buy or sell. It simply explains: "This event is linked to your portfolio because you currently hold TCS, representing 22% of your exposure, and it is a core condition in your Momentum Breakout Playbook."

## 4. Seamless Workflow
From the event panel, you can open the Decision Timeline (MVP-31) to see if this event forced a material shift in your exposure, or immediately launch the Research Engine (MVP-18) to begin testing a new hypothesis based on the earnings surprise.
