# MVP-30 Walkthrough: Portfolio & Strategy Allocation Intelligence

## 1. 4 Strategies ≠ 4 Independent Edges
Welcome to Portfolio Intelligence. You might think you are diversified because you run four different strategies. TradeFlow exposes the truth: if three of those strategies all buy IT stocks during high-volatility breakouts, you actually have massive concentration risk. 

## 2. Strategy Overlap Map
The new dashboard visually maps your strategy dependencies. You can instantly see how much of your portfolio's P&L is dependent on overlapping timing windows, shared market regimes, or identical underlying assets. 

## 3. Strategy Conflict & Capital Contention
If your "Mean Reversion" strategy generates a short signal on RELIANCE, but your "Momentum Breakout" strategy is heavily long, TradeFlow flags the **Strategy Conflict**. Furthermore, if your active playbooks require ₹55,000 to execute their rules but you only have ₹40,000 available, the system alerts you to **Capital Contention**.

## 4. Closing the Loop
When overlapping drawdowns are detected across your portfolio, TradeFlow automatically generates a Research Question ("Does strategy diversification actually reduce portfolio drawdown?"). This feeds directly back into the Research Engine and Strategy Lab, completing the ultimate learning loop.
